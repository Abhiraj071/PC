Legal Metrology Act, 2009 & Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011 — Key ProvisionsPurpose & ScopeThe Legal Metrology Act, 2009 establishes and enforces standards of weights and measures, and regulates trade and commerce in goods sold or distributed by weight, measure, or number. It aims to ensure accuracy, transparency, and fairness in transactions involving packaged commodities, protecting consumers from under-measurement, misleading declarations, and unfair trade practices. The Act came into force on 1 April 2011, replacing the earlier Weights and Measures legislation.
