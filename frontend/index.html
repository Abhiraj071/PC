Legal Metrology Knowledge Base

Official rules, consumer rights, and declaration guidelines

Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011

Rule 6 requires every packaged commodity to display Manufacturer Name & Address, Generic Name, Net Quantity, MRP (incl. taxes), Month/Year of Mfg, Consumer Care details, and Country of Origin on the principal display panel.

Font Size Specifications

Minimum font heights are mandatory based on principal display panel surface area: up to 100cm² requires ≥1mm; 100-500cm² requires ≥2mm; 500-1000cm² requires ≥3mm; >1000cm² requires ≥5mm.
